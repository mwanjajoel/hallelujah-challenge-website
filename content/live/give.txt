Dear Beloved,

We are immensely grateful to the Lord for the privilege to gather globally in praise, worship, and fervent prayer. As a ministry committed to godly integrity and absolute biblical stewardship, we ensure that every contribution received is deployed strictly and transparently into advancing the gospel.
How Contributions Are Utilized
01
Facilitating the HC & Year-Round Operations
Production venues, 4K multi-cam broadcast gear, musical instruments, team sustenance, robust streaming bandwidth, personnel remuneration, and critical administrative operations.
02
Team Honorarium & Volunteer Welfare
Supporting the dedicated production crew: videographers, sound engineers, screen mappers, graphic designers, photographers, web managers, Zoom & prayer facilitators, security, and Guest Ministers.
*Explicitly exclusive of Pastor Nathaniel Bassey.
03
Kingdom Creatives & Worship Platforms
Directly encouraging and equipping emerging psalmists, gospel artists, and kingdom worship altars dedicated to advancing sincere, scriptural praise across the nations.
04
Christ-Centred Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)
Demonstrating tangible Christ-like compassion through welfare for the underprivileged, clearance of medical bills for the sick, and societal benevolence under the injunction to "do good to all men".
Important Biblical Clarification on Tithes: The Hallelujah Challenge does not receive Tithes. Tithes belong strictly to the local church in which you are planted, discipled, and spiritually fed.
By making donations to the Hallelujah Challenge (through the registered entity – Hallelujah Challenge Gospel Music Initiative), you are undertaking that you have read and understood the above and that you are making any donation voluntarily from your lawful income without any compulsion or solicitation from Pastor Nathaniel Bassey or any other person associated with the Hallelujah Challenge.
