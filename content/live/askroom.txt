Welcome to the Ask Room
Every Hallelujah Challenge session is heralded by intense prayers, fasting, and sometimes, praise. Sessions are also closed with thanksgiving because, in the words of the Convener - Nathaniel Bassey - '
he who takes the glory, fights the battle'.
We call that prayer group 'Ask Room'. It consists of volunteer intercessors who labour in the place of prayer and fasting to implore heaven for the release of grace and power, for abounding signs and wonders to accompany every Hallelujah Challenge session.
