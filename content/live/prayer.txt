OCTOBER 2026 EDITION — PRAYER GUIDE
PRAYER DURATION
25 Minutes — Praying in Spiritual Tongues

A. THANKSGIVING
Scriptures: Psalm 136; Psalm 92
Father, in the name of Jesus, we thank You for Your goodness and faithfulness. We thank You for Your marvelous and wondrous works throughout every edition of the Hallelujah Challenge, from its inception in 2017 until this present day.
Father, we thank You for the salvation of souls and for the finished work of Jesus Christ on the cross.
Father, we thank You for every vessel You have used in the previous editions of the Hallelujah Challenge. We give You all the glory for their lives, service, sacrifices, and obedience.
B. THE BLOOD OF JESUS
Scriptures: Revelation 12:11; Hebrews 12:24
Father, in the name of Jesus, we come by the blood of Jesus.
Father, we plead the blood of Jesus over the entire event, every vessel, and every instrument that will be used throughout every night of this edition of the Hallelujah Challenge.
Father, let the power in the blood of Jesus be released every night of the Hallelujah Challenge.
C. THE HOLY SPIRIT / THE BREATH OF GOD
Scriptures: Job 33:4; Ezekiel 37:9; Isaiah 32:15; Acts 1:8; Acts 2:1–4
Father, in the name of Jesus, let there be a mighty move of Your Spirit throughout and after this event.
Father, because it is the Spirit who gives life, we ask for the Breath of God upon every session, every song, every prayer, and every prophetic utterance.
Father, we declare that the gifts of the Spirit will manifest in full measure, with precision and power.
Father, we declare an outpouring of Your Spirit upon every participant of this edition of the Hallelujah Challenge.
D. MERCY
Scriptures: Psalm 90:14; Psalm 85:7; Psalm 102:13
Father, in the name of Jesus, in this edition of the Hallelujah Challenge and in this season, satisfy us early with Your mercy, that we may rejoice and be glad all our days.
Father, in the name of Jesus, in this edition of the Hallelujah Challenge and in this season, show us Your mercy and grant us Your salvation.
Father, in the name of Jesus, arise and have mercy upon us; for the time to favour us, yes, the set time, has come.
Father, in the name of Jesus, let Your mercy break forth and let Your mercy break out in this edition of the Hallelujah Challenge, speaking powerfully into the lives of Your people.
E. THE FIRE OF GOD
Scripture: Acts 2:3–4
Father, in the name of Jesus, let Your fire fall every night of this edition of the Hallelujah Challenge.
F. HEALING STREAM
Scripture: Ezekiel 47:8–10 (NKJV)
Father, in the name of Jesus, let Your healing stream flow every night of this edition of the Hallelujah Challenge.
G. SIGNS AND WONDERS
Scriptures: Acts 4:16; Acts 5:12; Acts 10:38
Father, in the name of Jesus, we call forth testimonies, notable miracles, signs, and wonders; deliverances and breakthroughs like never seen before; and diverse and multiple healings.
H. SALVATION OF SOULS
Scriptures: Acts 2:47; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; Acts 2:41; Acts 4:4
Father, in the name of Jesus, we call forth a massive harvest of souls in this edition of the Hallelujah Challenge.
Father, let multitudes encounter Jesus Christ, receive salvation, and become established in the faith.
I. ANOINTING FOR THE MINISTERS
Scriptures: Acts 4:33; Psalm 92:10; Luke 3:16
Father, in the name of Jesus, we call forth fresh fire, fresh oil, great grace, and power for every minister in this edition of the Hallelujah Challenge.
Father, let every minister minister under the fullness of Your Spirit, with grace, accuracy, boldness, and power.
J. SUPERNATURAL STRENGTH
Scriptures: Ephesians
