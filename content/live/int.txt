When is the Hallelujah Festival?
The Hallelujah Festival will take place on Friday, October 30, 2026. It will be a vigil, and details regarding door opening and the commencement time will be released shortly.

The Hallelujah Festival will be held in Ikeja, Lagos, Nigeria.

No. Registration for the Festival does not guarantee a visa or provide immigration assistance, visa approval or entry into Nigeria.
